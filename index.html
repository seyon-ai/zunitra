Our Heritage

177 Years of
Uncompromising
Precision

In 1847, Jules Harmon established his first workshop in the Vallée de Joux — a valley of watchmakers where silence was sacred and imperfection unthinkable. Today, twelve generations of craftsmen carry that legacy forward, using techniques unchanged since the Second Empire.

Every Zunitra begins as a block of metal and a promise. It ends as an heirloom.

The Craft

Inside the Atelier

⚙

Movement Assembly

Each calibre is assembled by a single watchmaker over weeks of meticulous work. No component is rushed. No shortcut taken.

✦

Dial Artistry

Our dials are formed from extraordinary materials — meteorite, onyx, Grand Feu enamel — each requiring specialist artisans trained for decades.

◇

Case Finishing

Alternating between polished and satin surfaces demands absolute precision. A single case can take 80 hours of hand-finishing before leaving the atelier.

∞

Quality Control

Every watch undergoes 300 hours of testing before shipment. We reject anything that fails to exceed our standard — which itself exceeds every official certification.
