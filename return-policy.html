Overview

ZUNITRA offers a 3-day return window from the date of delivery. To be eligible, items must be unused, unworn, and returned in their original packaging with all accessories, certificates, and documentation included.

Returns are accepted for defective items, items that do not match their description, or items damaged in transit. We do not accept returns for items that show signs of wear, physical damage caused by the customer, or items returned without prior authorization.

How to Initiate a Return
1

Contact Us Within 3 Days

Email us at contact.zunitrawatches@gmail.com within 3 days of receiving your order. Include your Order ID, photos of the item, and the reason for return.

2

Receive Return Authorisation

Our team will review your request within 24 hours and send you a Return Authorisation (RA) number and return shipping instructions.

3

Pack & Ship the Item

Pack the item securely in its original box with all accessories, documentation, and warranty cards. Write your RA number on the outside of the package.

4

Refund Processed

Once we receive and inspect the returned item (2–3 business days), your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Refund Information

Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method used at checkout. Please allow 5–7 business days after we receive the return for the refund to reflect in your account.

Shipping charges: Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong item, defective product, or damage in transit). Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the item is faulty or incorrectly described.

For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds will be processed via bank transfer. Please provide your bank account details when contacting us.

Warranty

All ZUNITRA timepieces come with a manufacturer's warranty as specified on the individual product page (typically 1–2 years). Warranty covers manufacturing defects and movement issues under normal use conditions.

Warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage beyond the specified resistance rating, or damage caused by improper use, unauthorised service, or accidents.

Warranty claims: Contact us at contact.zunitrawatches@gmail.com with your proof of purchase and a description of the issue. We will coordinate with the manufacturer on your behalf.
